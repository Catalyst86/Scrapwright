# Scrapwright

You are a scrappy golden puppy with a red bandana, fighting for survival across 84 waves of increasingly dangerous enemies in a junkyard underworld.

## What is it?

A top-down arena survivors/bullet-heaven roguelite where you:

- **Bite enemies** with auto-attacks and collect **orbital weapons** that orbit around you
- **Dodge** through danger with a flame-trail dash that damages enemies in your path
- **Dig traps** that stun and damage enemies who walk over them
- **Sneak** for ambush attacks that deal double damage
- Collect **materials and keys** to open chests between waves
- Return to your **Den** between stages to spend materials on permanent upgrades

## Features

- **84 waves** across 6 unique biomes (Scrapyard, Fungal Depths, Molten Core, Frozen Caverns, Clockwork Factory, The Abyss)
- **12 boss fights** with multi-phase attack patterns
- **12 orbital weapons** that orbit and auto-fire (lightning, fire, frost, poison, blades, and more)
- **16 perks** with diminishing returns — every run plays differently
- **11 wearable armors** with unique pixel art animations
- **15 permanent upgrades** across 5 categories
- **Card collection** system with deck completion bonuses
- **16 achievements** to unlock
- **Full keybinding customization**
- **Gamepad support**

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Move | WASD | Left Stick |
| Dodge | Space | A |
| Bark | E | X |
| Dig/Salvage | F | Y |
| Sneak | Ctrl | LB |
| Collect | Shift | RB |
| Pause | ESC | Start |

All keybindings are rebindable from the pause menu.

## Early Access

This is an active development build. Feedback welcome! More armors, balance tweaks, and features are in progress.
