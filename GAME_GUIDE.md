# SCRAPWRIGHT — Field Manual
## A Puppy's Guide to Junkyard Survival

## CONTROLS

| Key | Action | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| **WASD** / Arrows | Move | 8-directional movement |
| **Space** | Bark | Manual attack — damages nearest enemy |
| **Shift** | Dodge Leap | Dash forward with invincibility (1.5s cooldown) |
| **Ctrl** (hold) | Sneak | Move at half speed — enemies lose track of you |
| **F** / Left Click | Dig / Salvage | Combat: dig a trap hole. Chest phase: salvage props |

## ABILITIES

### Auto-Attack (Bite)
Your puppy automatically bites the nearest enemy within range every 0.6 seconds. No input needed — just get close! The Chew Toy upgrade increases bite damage by +3 per level.

### Dodge Leap (Shift)
Dash in your facing direction with a burst of speed. You are completely **invincible** during the leap — projectiles, explosions, and contact damage pass right through you.
- **Duration:** 0.3 seconds
- **Cooldown:** 1.5 seconds
- **Best for:** Escaping exploder blasts, dodging projectiles, repositioning

### Sneak Mode (Hold Ctrl)
Crouch low and move at half speed. Enemies further than 30 pixels away lose track of you and stop chasing. Your first attack after sneaking deals **double damage** (Ambush Bite). Sneak has a limited duration — use it wisely!
- **Duration:** 4 seconds max
- **Cooldown:** Up to 5 seconds (proportional — short sneak = short cooldown, minimum 2s)
- **Speed:** 50% of normal
- **Detection range:** Enemies only notice you within 30px
- **Ambush bonus:** 2x damage on first hit — resets when attack connects or you take damage
- **Best for:** Quick repositioning behind tanks, setting up ambush damage, brief escape from groups

### Dig Holes (F key during combat)
Dig a trap hole in the ground. Takes 1.6 seconds (you can't move while digging). Enemies that walk over the hole fall in — **stunned for 3 seconds** and taking 15 damage.
- **Charges:** 2 per wave (base) + 1 per Buried Stash level
- **Duration:** 1.6 second channel
- **Interrupt:** Getting hit cancels the dig (charge is lost!)
- **Best for:** Chokepoints near spawn edges, slowing tanks, creating safe zones

## DOGGY DEN UPGRADES

Upgrade your base between runs at the Doggy Den. Each upgrade has 3 levels.

| Upgrade | Effect | Strategy |
|---------|--------|----------|
| **Toy Box** | Unlocks crafting recipes | Get this first to access better tools |
| **Chew Toy** | +3 bite damage per level | Direct power increase — strong at all stages |
| **Food Bowl** | +2 organic per run per level | Passive income for crafting |
| **Dog House** | Health regen during combat | Lv1: +1HP/8s, Lv2: +2HP/6s, Lv3: +3HP/4s |
| **Buried Stash** | +2 iron per run + dig charges | More trap holes per wave! |

## KEYS AND CHESTS

Enemies drop keys when defeated: **Bronze**, **Silver**, **Gold**, or **Secret**.
After each wave, chests spawn that require matching keys to open.

| Chest | Contents |
|-------|----------|
| **Bronze** | 2-4 basic materials |
| **Silver** | 3-5 materials, chance for +5 max HP |
| **Gold** | 4-6 materials, chance for permanent upgrade |
| **Secret** | 6-10 materials + guaranteed blueprint |

## TIPS AND COMBOS

1. **Ambush Combo:** Sneak (Ctrl) near a tank -> release -> first bite does 2x damage
2. **Hole Trap:** Dig holes near arena edges where enemies spawn — rushers walk right in
3. **Dodge Through:** Leap through a cluster of enemies to reach safety on the other side
4. **Dig + Sneak:** Dig a hole, then sneak away — enemies walk into the trap while ignoring you
5. **Exploder Escape:** When you see the fuse light up, immediately Shift-dodge away
6. **Dog House + Regen Perk:** These stack! Lv3 Dog House + Slow Regen perk = +5 HP every 4-5 seconds
7. **Buried Stash for Diggers:** Each Buried Stash level gives +1 dig charge per wave AND +2 iron per run
8. **Save Often:** Hit the SAVE button in the Doggy Den before starting a run
